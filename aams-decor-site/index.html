What we do

Décor built around your occasion, not a catalogue.

Every space and celebration is styled from scratch, in a palette and shape that fits the day — never a fixed package pulled off a shelf.

Home styling

Soft refreshes for a living room, entryway, or bedroom — florals, textiles, and small furnishing touches that change how a room feels.

Event décor

Full-room design for weddings, engagements, and naming ceremonies — table settings, backdrops, aisles, and stage arrangements.

Party & balloon design

Birthday parties, gender reveals, and small gatherings — balloon garlands, arches, and centrepieces in your chosen colour story.

Bespoke installations

Large-scale balloon walls, floral arches, and statement pieces for launches, brand activations, and milestone celebrations.
